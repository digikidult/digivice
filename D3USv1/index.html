01 / THE BASICS

Pick a partner. Start walking.

Controls, daily play and battle essentials / 玩法

Controls / 按鍵

► ：選擇 (A)| 右上鍵：確定(B) | 右下鍵：取消 (C)

► : Select (A) | Upper Right : Execute (B) |Lower Right: Cancel (C)

設置靜音 : 同時按A+B Mute: Press A and B simultaneously.

Start walking / 開始遊玩

選擇你的初始顆伴(V仔獸/麻鷹獸/迪露獸/穿山獸/巴達獸)，然後開始步行(搖機) Choose your initial partner(Veemon/Hawkmon/Armadillomon/Patamon/Tailmon), then start walking(shaking).

Everyday menu / 一般選單

States（狀態）：查看數碼暴龍及其進化之Life及Attack數值，最後查看之數碼暴龍會作為步行顆伴。 Check the Life and Attack stats of your Digimon and their evolution. And select your active partner for the last Digimon you check. Map（地圖）：共3關，必須順序闖關，不能跳關 Total 3 locations in the game in a fixed order, and you cannot switch between them until you have completed the game DigiEgg（數碼裝甲）：查看目前擁有的數碼裝甲。 Check the DigiEgg you currently have. Slot game（老虎機）：轉動輪盤以抽選兩隻數碼暴龍並此結果，以減少或增加關卡距離。 Spin the wheels to get two Digimon, and gain or lose distance depending on what was drawn. Medical（藥）：受傷時使用 Use this option when you got an injured Digimon. V.S.（對戰）：與其他暴龍機對戰 Connect and battle with another Digivice/Vpet;

Steps & D-power / 步數及DP

在伙伴步行畫面，按B鍵可查看 Press B button on the walking screen to check:

Distance( 距離 ): - 本關餘下所需步數 Distance remaining on the current map Steps( 步數 ): - 目前已步行的總數 The total number of steps taken D-power: - 進化所需的能量 (成熟期消耗8DP; 完全體消耗12DP ; 究極體需16DP) Energy for Evolutions 8 DP for Champion, 12 DP for Ultimate, and 16 DP for Mega;) - 最大99 ; 每100步增加1DP Max. value:99; Add 1DP every 100 steps 勝率 Winning ratios: - 對戰勝利百分比 Win percentage ratios against other’s Digivice when we battling

Walking events / 隨機事件

1. 普通戰鬥 Normal Battle

- 步行時，每約400-600步便會出現敵人 When walking, you can be attacked by enemy Digimon for every 400-600 steps

2. 數碼裝甲 DigiEgg

- 反覆按B鍵直到達到4格。成功的話，將隨機獲得數碼裝甲 或 失散的顆伴 Press the B button repeatedly until it reaches 4 bars. If you succeed, you will get a DigiEgg randomly OR a lost partner.

Battle rules / 戰鬥相關

- 每回合有四輪攻擊，以搖機之結果(POW)決定攻擊組合(空炮/有效攻擊)，攻擊力較高的一方能成功攻擊對方 如雙方攻擊力相同，則攻擊該輪會相互抵消。 Each round consists of four turns of attacks, and the combination of null/vaild attacks is determined by the result of your shakes (POW). The side with the higher attack power, will successfully attacks the opponent. If both sides have equal attack power, the attacks of this turn will cancel each other out. -勝負機制：任何一方之數碼暴龍陣亡，即告負 Winning condition: Digimon on either side is defeated, it results in a loss. - 戰勝獎勵: 有機會得到額外DP Victory Reward: Chance to receive extra DP - 戰敗時：距離將額外增加500步。 Defeat in Battle: Results in a penalty of 500 points added to your distance.

Battle menu / 戰鬥選單

戰鬥選單 BATTLE MENU FIGHT(戰鬥): 在出現"Count"時搖機，以搖出之POW決定攻擊組合 When "Count" appears, shake the device and the combination of attacks will be determined by the resulting POW value. DigiEgg (數碼裝甲): 裝甲進化用，無須使用DP(但戰鬥結束時，有一定機率出現裝甲損壞) For Armor Evolutions, no DP required. (But a chance of armor damage at the end of battles.) DIGIVOLVE (進化): [在擊敗所在地區的中Boss前，此項會被隱藏 This will be hidden until the miniboss of the current area is defeated. ] 常規進化用 - 成熟期消耗8DP; 完全體消耗12DP ; 究極體需16DP For Normal Evolutions - 8 DP for Champion, 12 DP for Ultimate, and 16 DP for Mega PARTY (換人): 戰鬥中變更顆伴 Change your partner digimon for the fight ESC (逃走): 逃離戰鬥 Escape from the current battle
