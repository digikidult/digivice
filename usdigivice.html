按鍵指南 | Buttons

• 左鍵：確定 (B) | 右上鍵：選擇 (A) | 右下鍵：取消 (C)
• Left: Confirm (B) | Upper Right: Select (A) | Lower Right: Cancel (C)

開始遊玩 | Get Started

選擇你的初始夥伴然後開始步行 (搖機)
Select your partner Digimon, then start walking (shaking).

選單 | Menu

  • States（狀態）：查看數碼暴龍及其進化之Life及Attack數值，最後查看之數碼暴龍會作為步行夥伴。
    Check the Life and Attack stats of your Digimon and their evolution. And select your active partner for the last Digimon you check.
  • Map（地圖）：共7關，必須順序闖關，不能跳關。
    Total 7 locations in the game in a fixed order, and you cannot switch between them until you have completed the game.
  • Slot game（老虎機）：轉動輪盤以抽選兩隻數碼暴龍並此結果，以減少或增加關卡距離。
    Spin the wheels to get two Digimon, and gain or lose distance depending on what was drawn.
  • Card game（卡牌遊戲）：此遊戲使用「確定」鍵代表左邊的卡牌，「選擇」鍵代表右邊的卡牌。兩張卡牌上有數碼暴龍，你有很短的時間可以按下其中一個、兩個，或不按。對於每一方，如果卡牌是敵方數碼暴龍，按下按鈕進行攻擊；如果卡牌是夥伴，則不應按下按鈕。這個步驟會重複四次，最終根據你的表現增加或減少距離。
    You use the OK button for the left card and the Select button for the right card. Two Digimon cards are drawn, and you have a short time to press one, both, or neither button. Press the button to attack if the card represents an enemy Digimon, and don't press it if it represents a partner. This is repeated four times, and your performance determines whether you gain or lose distance.
  • Medical（藥）：受傷時使用。
    Use this option when you got an injured Digimon.
  • V.S.（對戰）：與其他暴龍機對戰。
    Connect and battle with another Digivice/Vpet.

查看步數及DP | Check Steps & D-power

在夥伴步行畫面，按B鍵可查看 (Press B button on the walking screen to check):
  • Distance (距離): 本關餘下所需步數 (Distance remaining on the current location)
  • Steps (步數): 目前已步行的總數 (The total number of steps taken)
  • D-power: 進化所需的能量 (成熟期消耗3DP; 完全體消耗6DP ; 時空進化究極體需9DP；戰敗也會扣減2DP)
    Energy for Evolutions: 3 DP for Adult/Champion, 6 DP for Perfect/Ultimate, and 9 DP for warp evolutions to Mega; Deduct 2DP when losing
    - 最大99 ; 每100步增加1DP (Max. value:99 ; Add 1DP every 100 steps)

夥伴加入 | Digimon Recruitable

另外7隻夥伴也會在冒險中加入隊伍，如戰鬥後隨機地出現求救畫面 (夥伴黑影)，連按A鍵即可獲得相應夥伴。
You will be able to have the other 7 Digimon join your team by pressing the A button rapidly when the Help Wave (shadow of the partner) appears randomly after battle.

戰鬥相關 | About Battle

  • 步行時，每約300-500步便會出現敵人 (When walking, you can be attacked by enemy Digimon for every 300-500 steps)
  • 當到達每張地圖終點時，會出現Boss戰 (You will be attacked by a Boss when you reach the end of each area.)
  • Boss戰有5回合，一般敵人則為3回合 (Boss battles have 5 turns. Regular enemies only have 3 turns.)
  • 所有回合結束後，以血量高低判定勝負，平手也算我方勝 (After all turns are completed, victory or loss is determined based on the remaining Life level of each side. It also counts as player's victory for if both sides have the same Life.)

戰鬥操作 | Battle Flow

  • FIGHT (戰鬥): 在出現 "Press" 時連按 A 鍵 (Attacks are registered by pressing the A button rapidly when showing 'Press')
  • DIGIVOLVE (進化): 可直接搖機當出現 "Count" (Shaking the Digivice in the right way when it says "Count")
    [V2/V2.5] 當已解鎖時空進化，需選擇「一般進化」/「時空進化」("Warp"/ "Help")才開始搖機進化 (When unlocked Warp Evolutions, have to select "Normal" OR "Warp"/ "Help" before shaking)
  • PARTY (換人): 戰鬥中變更夥伴 (Change your partner digimon for the fight)
  • ESC (逃走): 逃離戰鬥 (Escape from the current battle)

時空進化 (只限012 & 0125) | Warp Evolutions (ver2 & ver 2.5 only)

觸發條件 | How to unlock

  1. 達 50,000 步 (Over 50,000 steps)
  2. 高勝率 High % of win rate (>90%?)

如何成功時空進化 | How to be warp evolved successfully?

  • 只搖機 3 / 5 / 7 下 (Shake 3 / 5 / 7 times only)
  • * 沒有究極體的夥伴使用時空進化，如成功會召喚黃金劍獅獸 (012 & 0125) 或 飛獅獸 (012) / 神龍獸 (0125) 幫忙。
    (Partners without ultimate form, will call Saberleomon(v2 & v2.5) OR Griffomon(v2) / Goddramon(v2.5) as helper when the warp evolution is successful.)

成功時空進化後 | When warp evolution successfully

  • 對戰一般敵人時將會自動攻擊 (During battles with normal enemies, automatic attacks will be initiated.)
  • 此後 3 場戰鬥無需搖機自動進化究極體 (Boss戰不適用)
    For the next three battles, it will automatically evolve to Mega (not applicable for Boss battles).
