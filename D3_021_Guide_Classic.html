按鍵指南 | Navigations

  • ► ：選擇 (A) | Select (A)
  • 右上鍵：確定 (B) | Upper Right : Execute (B)
  • 右下鍵：取消 (C) | Lower Right: Cancel (C)
  • 設置靜音 : 同時按 A+B | Mute: Press A and B simultaneously.

開始遊玩 | Get Started

選擇你的初始夥伴（V仔獸 / 麻鷹獸 / 穿山獸 / 巴達獸 / 迪露獸），然後開始步行（搖機）。

Choose your initial partner (Veemon / Hawkmon / Armadillomon / Patamon / Tailmon), then start walking (shaking).

一般選單 | NON-BATTLE MENU

  • States（狀態）：查看數碼暴龍及其進化之 Life 及 Attack 數值，最後查看之數碼暴龍會作為步行夥伴。
    Check the Life and Attack stats of your Digimon and their evolution. And select your active partner for the last Digimon you check.
  • Map（地圖）：共 3 關，必須順序闖關，不能跳關。
    Total 3 locations in the game in a fixed order, and you cannot switch between them until you have completed the game.
  • DigiEgg（數碼裝甲）：查看目前擁有的數碼裝甲。
    Check the DigiEgg you currently have.
  • Slot game（老虎機）：轉動輪盤以抽選兩隻數碼暴龍並依此結果，以減少或增加關卡距離。
    Spin the wheels to get two Digimon, and gain or lose distance depending on what was drawn.
  • Medical（藥）：受傷時使用。
    Use this option when you got an injured Digimon.
  • V.S.（對戰）：與其他暴龍機對戰。
    Connect and battle with another Digivice/Vpet.

查看步數及DP | Check Steps & D-power

在伙伴步行畫面，按 B 鍵可查看。 Press B button on the walking screen to check:

  • Distance（距離）：本關餘下所需步數。 Distance remaining on the current map.
  • Steps（步數）：目前已步行的總數。 The total number of steps taken.
  • D-power：進化所需的能量（成熟期消耗 8DP；完全體消耗 12DP；究極體需 16DP）。 Energy for Evolutions: 8 DP for Champion, 12 DP for Ultimate, and 16 DP for Mega. 最大 99；每 100 步增加 1DP。 Max. value: 99; Add 1DP every 100 steps.
  • 勝率 Winning ratios：對戰勝利百分比。 Win percentage ratios against other’s Digivice when battling.

隨機事件 | Events while walking

1. 普通戰鬥 Normal Battle

步行時，每約 400-600 步便會出現敵人。
When walking, you can be attacked by enemy Digimon for every 400-600 steps.

2. 數碼裝甲 DigiEgg

反覆按 B 鍵直到達到 4 格。成功的話，將隨機獲得數碼裝甲 或 失散的夥伴。
Press the B button repeatedly until it reaches 4 bars. If you succeed, you will get a DigiEgg randomly OR a lost partner.

戰鬥相關 | About Battle

  • 每回合有四輪攻擊，以搖機之結果（POW）決定攻擊組合（空炮 / 有效攻擊），攻擊力較高的一方能成功攻擊對方。如雙方攻擊力相同，則攻擊該輪會相互抵消。
    Each round consists of four turns of attacks, and the combination of null/valid attacks is determined by the result of your shakes (POW). The side with the higher attack power will successfully attack the opponent. If both sides have equal attack power, the attacks of this turn will cancel each other out.
  • 勝負機制：任何一方之數碼暴龍陣亡，即告負。
    Winning condition: Digimon on either side is defeated, it results in a loss.
  • 戰勝獎勵：有機會得到額外 DP。
    Victory Reward: Chance to receive extra DP.
  • 戰敗時：距離將額外增加 500 步。
    Defeat in Battle: Results in a penalty of 500 points added to your distance.

戰鬥選單 | BATTLE MENU

  • FIGHT（戰鬥）：在出現 "Count" 時搖機，以搖出之 POW 決定攻擊組合。
    When "Count" appears, shake the device and the combination of attacks will be determined by the resulting POW value.
  • DigiEgg（數碼裝甲）：裝甲進化用，無須使用 DP（但戰鬥結束時，有一定機率出現裝甲損壞）。
    For Armor Evolutions, no DP required. (But a chance of armor damage at the end of battles.)
  • DIGIVOLVE（進化）：在擊敗所在地區的中 Boss 前，此項會被隱藏。常規進化用 — 成熟期消耗 8DP；完全體消耗 12DP；究極體需 16DP。
    This will be hidden until the miniboss of the current area is defeated. For Normal Evolutions — 8 DP for Champion, 12 DP for Ultimate, and 16 DP for Mega.
  • PARTY（換人）：戰鬥中變更夥伴。
    Change your partner Digimon for the fight.
  • ESC（逃走）：逃離戰鬥。
    Escape from the current battle.
